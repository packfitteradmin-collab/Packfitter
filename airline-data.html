Use the data

Free to use — just link back

This dataset is published under CC-BY 4.0: use it anywhere — blog posts, apps, spreadsheets, AI tools — as long as you credit PackFitter with a link. Bloggers and developers are welcome; this is meant to be the maintained source the web can cite instead of copying stale figures.

What changed

Changelog

  • Jun 2026 — Added 17 international carriers (Qatar, Etihad, Aer Lingus, Vueling, Norwegian, Eurowings, Virgin Atlantic, Iberia, SWISS, SAS, Finnair, Wizz Air, ANA, JAL, Korean Air, Hawaiian, Allegiant).
  • Jun 2026 — Standardized personal-item handling: airlines that don’t publish a size are marked “no published size” rather than asserting an estimate.
  • Mar 2026 — Aer Lingus aligned regional (Emerald) flights to the mainline 55 × 40 × 24 cm / 10 kg limit and now publishes a 40 × 30 × 20 cm personal item.
  • Mar 2026 — Frontier carry-on weight corrected to 35 lb (16 kg).
  • May 2026 — Spirit Airlines ceased operations; retained for reference, excluded from live tools.
