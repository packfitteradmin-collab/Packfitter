What to Pack for a 14-Day Trip

Last updated: April 2026

Packing for a 14-day trip with carry-on only is difficult without a deliberate strategy. This page covers what to pack, how much space it takes, and includes a calculator to check if your setup fits. Most 14-day setups need 40–55 liters — carry-on is only realistic with laundry access mid-trip and disciplined packing choices.

Quick Answer

Can You Pack for 14 Days in a Carry-On?

Only with laundry — otherwise, plan for a checked bag. With laundry every 4–5 days, a 40–45L carry-on becomes realistic — you're packing the same volume as a 7–10 day trip. Without laundry, clothing count alone pushes most setups past carry-on range.

Typical bag size: 40–45L for most people. The hardest variables are no laundry access, cold weather, and extra shoes.

    Challenges

    What Makes a 14-Day Trip Harder to Pack?

    Two weeks is where carry-on requires deliberate strategy. The biggest factors:

    • No laundry access — 14 days of clothing without washing almost always requires a checked bag
    • Cold weather layers — heavy outerwear and thermals add 5–10L on top of an already full bag
    • Extra shoes — a second pair adds 6–8L and creates packing geometry issues in a near-capacity bag
    • Laptop + full tech kit — adds 3–5L of rigid volume that can't be offset by clothing reduction
    • Multiple climate zones — trips spanning warm and cold weather require layering for both
    • Heavy packing style — at 14 days, every "just in case" item compounds dramatically

    Plan to wash every 4–5 days and pack only enough clothing for one wash cycle: 4–5 tops, 5–7 pairs of underwear and socks, 2–3 bottoms. Wear your bulkiest layer at the airport. Quick-dry fabrics help between laundromat stops. Travelers who commit to this approach pack the same volume as a one-week trip. Those who don't are looking at a checked bag almost every time.
