What to Pack for a 3-Day Trip

Last updated: April 2026

Packing for a 3-day trip is simple enough that most people skip planning — and often bring a larger bag than necessary. This page covers what to pack, how much space it takes, and includes a calculator to check if your setup fits your bag. Most 3-day setups fit comfortably in a 20–30L bag — which often means a personal item is enough and a full carry-on is optional.

Quick Answer

Can You Pack for 3 Days in a Carry-On?

Most 3-day setups fit in a personal item (20–25L) or small carry-on. You'd only need a full carry-on if you're adding a laptop, extra shoes, or cold-weather layers.

    Challenges

    What Makes a 3-Day Trip Harder to Pack?

    A 3-day trip is small enough that extras have an outsized impact on total volume:

    • Cold weather layers — a jacket, fleece, and thermal base add 5–10L, which can double the base clothing volume on a short trip
    • Extra shoes — a second pair of sneakers or boots adds 6–8L
    • Laptop + full tech kit — adds 3–5L of rigid, non-compressible volume
    • Bulky items — travel pillow, camera gear, or large toiletry bags
    • Heavy packing style — "just in case" items have a bigger proportional impact on short trips

    Wearing your bulkiest layer at the airport makes a bigger difference on short trips — fewer items are packed overall, so each bulky piece has a larger proportional impact. One compressible mid layer handles most cold-weather needs for a weekend.
