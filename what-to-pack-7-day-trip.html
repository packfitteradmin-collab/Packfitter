What to Pack for a 7-Day Trip

Last updated: April 2026

Packing for a 7-day trip is where many travelers either overpack or choose the wrong size bag. This page covers what to pack, how much space it takes, and includes a calculator that checks whether your setup actually fits your bag. Carry-on-focused 7-day setups typically fit a 35–40L bag — but the real variable is climate, packing style, and whether you have laundry access mid-trip.

Quick Answer

Can You Pack for 7 Days in a Carry-On?

With laundry mid-trip, a 35–40L carry-on works well. Without laundry, you'll need a 40–45L bag and disciplined packing to stay within carry-on limits.

    Challenges

    What Makes a 7-Day Trip Harder to Pack?

    Seven days is where carry-on starts getting tight. The biggest volume drivers at this trip length:

    • No laundry access — packing a full 7 days of clothing is the single biggest volume increase over shorter trips
    • Cold weather layers — a jacket, fleece, and thermal base add 5–10L on top of an already full bag
    • Extra shoes — a second pair adds 6–8L and creates packing geometry issues
    • Laptop + full tech kit — adds 3–5L of rigid, non-compressible volume
    • Bulky items — travel pillow, camera gear, or large toiletry bags
    • Heavy packing style — at 7 days, "just in case" items compound significantly

    Laundry is the biggest lever: even one mid-trip wash lets you pack 4 tops instead of 7 and cut underwear and socks by nearly half — saving 5–8L. Wear your bulkiest layer at the airport. If you're adding a laptop and extra shoes on top of 7 days of clothing, you're likely pushing past 40L — still carry-on compliant, but with very little margin.
